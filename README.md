# HUMASTRA | Strategic Executive Search & Leadership Advisory

A modern, high-trust digital platform for **HUMASTRA**, a premier executive search and leadership advisory firm advising boards, founders, and growth-stage enterprises on high-stakes CXO appointments and organizational governance.

## Practice Areas

* **Executive & CXO Search:** Retained partner-led search for Chief Executive Officers, Chief Financial Officers, Chief Technology Officers, Chief Operating Officers, and Board Directors.
* **Confidential Search:** Discreet replacement mandates with strict non-disclosure safeguards.
* **Strategic Leadership Advisory:** Executive team design, founder-to-scale evolution, and succession architectures.
* **Talent Intelligence & Market Mapping:** Exhaustive ecosystem analysis and competitive talent benchmarking.

## Key Features

* **Design Aesthetic:** Editorial serif typography (Newsreader) paired with Plus Jakarta Sans, refined champagne bronze accents, and minimal corporate luxury.
* **Technical SEO:** 100% semantic HTML5, canonical URLs, XML sitemap, robots.txt, and complete Schema.org JSON-LD (Organization, WebSite, Person, Article, BreadcrumbList).
* **Zero Dependencies:** Pure semantic HTML5, modern vanilla CSS tokens, and lightweight accessible JavaScript.
* **Founder & Leadership:** Verified public profile of Founder & CEO **Himanshu Nandecha** with corporate governance disclosures and direct advisory channels.
