Executive Background

Bridging Operational Talent Mastery with Corporate Board Governance

Himanshu Nandecha is the Founder and CEO of HUMASTRA. His career is defined by a commitment to strategic talent acquisition, executive assessment, and organizational leadership alignment across growth and enterprise sectors.

Prior to founding HUMASTRA, Himanshu served as the Head of Talent Acquisitions at Topaz Multi Groups, leading comprehensive talent strategy, recruitment architecture, and specialized hiring across complex operational ecosystems in India. In this role, he was instrumental in diagnosing talent bottlenecks, refining candidate evaluation frameworks, and aligning talent acquisition directly with corporate business objectives.

In addition to his hands-on recruitment leadership, Himanshu brings direct corporate governance and fiduciary oversight experience as an Independent Non-Executive Director at Ajwa Fun World & Resorts Ltd. (listed on BSE India). His board service encompasses corporate governance, board committee participation, and advising on talent retention and organizational risk management within a publicly accountable environment.

"Recruitment becomes strategic only when an advisor understands both sides of the table—the operational demands of the hiring executive and the governance fiduciary duties of the board. HUMASTRA was founded to embody that dual perspective."

The Human Advisory Commitment

Throughout his career, Himanshu has maintained a human-centered philosophy of leadership mentorship. Through platforms such as Topmate (topmate.io/nandecha), he actively advises aspiring and established leaders on career direction, high-stakes interview preparation, and navigating career transitions.

This direct, ongoing engagement with exceptional professionals across the industry provides HUMASTRA with rare, authentic visibility into candidate motivations, career psychology, and leadership readiness—insights that dry database algorithms can never replicate.
